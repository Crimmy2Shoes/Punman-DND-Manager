## Spreadsheet-era ability guide (SRD 5.1)

This work includes material taken from the System Reference Document 5.1 (“SRD 5.1”) by Wizards of the Coast LLC and available at https://dnd.wizards.com/resources/systems-reference-document. The SRD 5.1 is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License available at https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.

The default spreadsheet companion extracts and reformats class/subclass features, racial traits, 319 spells, 32 invocations, 18 skills and equipment/magic-item definitions from the official SRD 5.1 PDF. Feature categories, level filtering and grant summaries are adaptations. The SRD 5.2.1 data above remains in the earlier 2024 journal.

Additional PHB and expansion spell/subclass entries are original mechanical summaries with links to the community World Anvil references and publisher material used for research. They do not reproduce full non-SRD book prose or claim those sources have SRD licensing. Expansion coverage and its boundaries are documented in `References/OFFICIAL_2014_SETUP.md`. Complex tables and summoned creature stat blocks retain linked references.

Pugilist mechanical summaries refer to the user-selected [World Anvil reference](https://www.worldanvil.com/block/205750), a separate homebrew source, and do not claim SRD licensing for that class.
